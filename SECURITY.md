# Security policy

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability, exposed credential, payment bypass, or private customer data.

Email `memerhuwhite@gmail.com` with:

- The affected AgentToll route or public artifact
- Reproduction steps that do not move real funds
- The expected and observed behavior
- Any request or response identifiers that do not contain secrets

Do not include wallet private keys, seed phrases, raw authorization headers, payment signatures, or customer data. Do not test against another person's wallet or account.

AgentToll does not run a paid vulnerability-reward program. Reporters should not incur costs or execute paid calls without written approval.
